Motion

How generated frames handle animation, patterns and on-screen text.

For drawn content: frames the animation repeats are not blended into the next drawing.

With Animation mode. Spreads animation drawn on twos and threes evenly over the frames each drawing is held for, so motion slides instead of stepping. Every shown frame is then generated; can look soft or wrong on fast action.

Re-checks motion on fences, railings and window grids, which can double during pans. Stronger with Animation mode. Small GPU cost.

Burnt-in subtitles, logos and HUDs are taken from the source frames instead of moving with the picture behind them.

Bicubic sampling for generated frames: crisper edges, hair and texture. About 0.2 ms per generated frame.
